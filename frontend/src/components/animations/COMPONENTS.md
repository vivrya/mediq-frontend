# Animation Components

Decorative SVG/canvas animations. All accept `aria-hidden` and are purely visual — they carry no semantic content.

---

## NeuralNet
Procedurally generated neural network SVG with pulsing nodes and connecting edges. Responds to mouse movement with a parallax translation effect. Used in: Hero background.

Props: `nodeCount` (default 22–26, randomised), `width`, `height`

## CellMitosis
SVG animation depicting cell division — a central cell splits into two daughter cells with spinning organelle rings and breathing/division keyframe animations. Used in: AdhdMethods section sidebar.

## OrbitParticles
Three concentric rings of particles orbiting a central orb, with per-particle twinkle animations. One ring spins in reverse for visual depth. Used in: FinalCTA section background.
